The Problem

Every Minute
Costs Lives

The Hartford Consensus principle — “no one should die from uncontrolled bleeding” — remains unimplemented. Current emergency systems are fundamentally broken.

TEMS Systems Are Critically Understaffed

Tactical Emergency Medical Support requires live medics who are themselves at risk. In mass incidents, casualties far outnumber available medics.

Ambulances Wait 20–60 Minutes

EMS cannot enter an area until police declare it safe. In active shooter scenarios this delay is often fatal — cardiac arrest survival drops 10% every minute.

Remote Areas Are Completely Uncovered

Floods, earthquakes, industrial accidents — victims wait hours with no response. Crush syndrome and hypothermia kill within 30 minutes of onset.
